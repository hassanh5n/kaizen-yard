# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (user chose Next.js/React for a premium feel). Built as a static export so it deploys anywhere (Vercel, Netlify, any static host).

## Users

Car buyers in Kingston and Montego Bay, Jamaica, mostly browsing on mobile phones, comparing used Japanese imports (Toyota, Honda, Nissan, Mazda) by price, mileage, engine size and whether the car is already in the yard or still on the water. Many revisit to check fresh stock.

## Product Purpose

Demo site for a Japanese used-car import dealership in Kingston, Jamaica. Its job is lead generation: turn a browsing visitor into a WhatsApp conversation or a booked test drive. Success = taps on "Chat on WhatsApp" and test-drive requests.

## Positioning

Direct Japan imports with verified auction grades, sold from a Kingston yard. Japanese spec data (stock/chassis ID, km, cc, RHD, transmission) is shown up front.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Pages: Home, Full Inventory, New Arrivals, How to Buy, About, Contact, single vehicle detail.
- Filters: make, model, year, price (USD/JMD), body type, availability (Kingston yard vs in transit), mileage, engine cc, transmission, steering (RHD).
- Primary CTA: bright green "Chat on WhatsApp" on every listing; sticky WhatsApp button top-right of header. Secondary CTA: "Book a Test Drive" modal form.
- Mobile-first: sticky search bar on mobile, touch-friendly image carousels, fast load.
- No backend: forms hand off to WhatsApp.

## Brand Commitments

Dealership name invented for the demo (user delegated): Kaizen Yard.

## Evidence on Hand

- Five required listings (2018 Vitz, 2017 Fit, 2019 Note e-Power, 2018 Probox, 2016 Vezel) with given JMD/USD prices. Two extra listings added by Claude (Axio sedan, Mazda Demio) so Sedan/Mazda filters return results.
- Photos: free-licensed Wikimedia Commons images of the actual models, credited on a credits page.
- No real testimonials, yard photos, address, phone or auction sheets exist: all are labeled sample content to replace.

## Product Principles

- Every listing leads to WhatsApp in one tap.
- Show the Japanese spec data buyers check first, before the sales copy.
- Fast on a mid-range Android phone on mobile data.
